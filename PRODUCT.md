## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. No framework, no build step (pinned by the briefing). Projects and reviews come from data files in `data/`.

## Users

Primary: professional clients. These are building companies, contractors and turnkey builders (sleutel-op-de-deur) who need an electrical subcontractor for their sites, plus companies, offices and industry that need installations. Most homes Boon wires are also contractor projects (B2B). Private homeowners are welcome but secondary.

The visitor's questions, in order: can Boon do this, have they done it before, how do I get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Elektriciteitswerken Boon (Boon bv, Lokeren) designs and installs electrical installations: general electrical work, home automation and central control (domotica), data and network cabling, and lighting. They work for homes, offices and industry in the Waasland region and beyond. The site exists to win B2B project work. Success means quote requests and calls from contractors and companies.

## Positioning

Experience in technical, complex installations and automation. It's a family company founded in 1983 by Robert Boon, with Robby Boon joining in 1995 (second generation) and incorporated as Boon bv in 2008. They have specialised in central control systems and domotica since the 1990s. They are a fixed partner for local contractors: planning matched to the site, one point of contact, and work done to the AREI regulations with schemas and inspection in order.

## Operating Context

Contractors evaluate subcontractors on reliability, references, planning and paperwork (AREI keuring, eendraadschema, situatieschema). Decisions often happen by phone. On mobile, the phone number must be one tap away.

## Product Principles

1. Answer the contractor's questions in order: capability, proof, contact.
2. Proof over claims: real projects with real specifics. Unknowns stay visibly unknown.
3. One primary action (quote request), with the phone always within reach.
4. Calm confidence. Every element must earn its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
